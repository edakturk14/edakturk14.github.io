About Me

I make technical products easier to understand, use, and adopt.

My career path has been engineering → technical sales → developer relations → partner engineering, with stops at the World Bank, GE Healthcare, Cisco, and Hyperlane.

I especially enjoy working with developer communities. That’s been my main focus for the past six years. I create technical content, run workshops, bring developers together, and help partners/customers build with the tools we make.

I’ve been writing online since 2021, now on Substack and previously on Paragraph and Hashnode.

You can find me terminally online on X.
